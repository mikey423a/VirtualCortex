Offline feasibility experiment against the 6.41 baseline cbd0aa6c8e87b33aafc8c556119636d85152f5b5. Build probe.cpp with g++ -std=c++17 -O2 and execute it. A mono sustained 200 Hz tone drives a feedback delay and a polarity-changing tap. Both live taps remain available during a 5 ms linear blend. The delay advances once per sample. The first switched sample changes by 0.51482 versus 0.00222847 at 44100 Hz, 0.515033 versus 0.00206067 at 48000 Hz, and 0.512005 versus 0.0010453 at 96000 Hz. The post-release tail has positive energy and the blended output exactly rejoins the selected tap at the endpoint. This fixture measures the first step of one transition. It is an isolated prototype, outside the Windows application and CI.
